How this was drawn The photos are cropped with object-fit: cover inside boxes of fixed proportions, so none is resampled to a different shape. Below about 460 px the collage turns into a grid — the overlapping layout would shrink the snapshots to thumbnails on a phone. Plants, building and icons are sized by CSS height. The pin, calendar, arrows and sparkles are inline SVG. The photos were re-encoded from the originals, which also drops any location data in them. 09 · The palms, building and price tag had their baked-in white fill keyed out (DESIGN.md ramp), all icons trimmed to their art and sized by CSS height. The rates are a real HTML <table>; the box, divider lines and rays are CSS/SVG. Below about 460 px the two info cells stack. Typography warning Norican, Dancing Script, DM Mono and Anonymous Pro are loaded from Google Fonts. Offline they fall back to system faces and the page looks wrong. Judge the type only on the deployed preview.
