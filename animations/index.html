Animations

Standalone pages, one motion idea each. Nothing here is wired into the site yet — they exist so a movement can be judged and tuned on its own before it lands in a stage.

Shared source, on purpose Each page reuses the generator already in the build it belongs to — wavePath() from stage1, wavyLine() from stage3 — copied verbatim. Only the reveal is new. If you change a generator in a stage, change it here too, or the demo stops telling the truth.

Line reveals

Wavy panel edge, self-drawing 27 Aug 2026

The cream timeline panel's wavy border drawn from a single point until it closes on itself, with the panel fill and the content fading in behind it. Sliders for wavelength, amplitude, corner radius and duration.

  • The look is one ratio: wavelength to amplitude, about 11 : 1 (len 190, amp 17 in stage1). Raise the amplitude and the wobble turns into a zigzag.
  • Wave count is rounded to a whole number, which is what lets the sine meet itself at the seam with no kink.
  • stroke-dashoffset is not GPU-composited — it repaints every frame. One path is fine; a dozen is not.
  • animations/wavy-frame-draw.html Panel edge reveal, with controls.
Wavy underline, self-drawing 27 Aug 2026

The rule under the section 07 heading drawn left to right, staggered across three headings so the timing can be judged as a group rather than one at a time.

  • Each rule regenerates at its heading's real width, so the bumps stay the same size and the count differs per heading. That is the intended behaviour, not a bug.
  • Needs document.fonts.ready before measuring — measured too early, the width is the fallback font's and every rule is wrong.
  • animations/wavy-underline-draw.html Heading underline reveal, with controls.

Open questions

  • Does the site want entrance animation at all? Nothing in the Canva set implies motion. This is an addition to the design, not a rebuild of it, and needs the same sign-off a copy or layout change would.
  • Reduced motion. Neither page honours prefers-reduced-motion yet. Anything that ships must, and the frame reveal in particular hides the panel until it finishes.
  • Where a reveal would trigger. On load, or on scroll into view via IntersectionObserver? Not decided.
